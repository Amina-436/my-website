MY FAVOURITE HOBBY

Book Reading

Introduction

Every person has something that makes them happy in their free time.Some people like to play games, some like to draw, and some like to travel.My favourite hobby is book reading.Whenever I get bored and have some free time,i pick up a good book and get lost in the world of stories.Reading is not just a pasttime for me.It is a calm and happy part of my life.

How my love for books began

My love for books started when i was young.I used to enjoy to listening to stories, and slowly I wanted to read them on my own.The first time I finished a whole book by myself,I felt very proud.From that day, books became my close friends. Each new book felt like a door to a new world,and i wanted to open as many doors as i could.

Why I love reading

  • Books give me new knowledge and fresh ideas.
  • Books make my imagination stronger.
  • It makes me more patient and thoughtful.
  • It reduces the stress from my life.
  • It improves my vacabloury and writing.

Kinds of books i enjoy

I enjoy many kinds of books.Story books and novels take me to new places and let me meet intresting characters.History books teach me about the people and events of the past.Books about great leaders and scientist inspire me to work hard.Motivational books remind me never to give up,even when things are difficult.Every kind of books teaches me something different,and that is why i never get bored.

What reading has taught me

Reading has taught me many valuable lessons.When i read about different characters,i learn that how people face problems and they how they find their solutions.I learn that honesty and hardwork are always important.Books have also taught me to see the world in other people's eyes, which helps me understand others better.These lessons help me in my own life everyday.

Benefits of Reading

Reading has many Benefits for the mind.It improves concentration because we have to focus the story.It builds a good vacabloury and helps us speak and write better.It also helps us in our studies,because a person who reads a lot can understand lessons more easily. Most importantly,reading gives peace to the hearts.After a titing day,a few pages book can make me feel fresh.

Sharing of the joy of reading

I believe that the joy of reading should be shared.I love to talk about the books I have read with my family and friends,and I enjoy hearing about the books they like.Sometimes we exchange books with each other.This way, we all get to read new stories without spending extra money, and we learn from each others thought too.

Conclusion

In Conclusion, book reading is my favourite hobby and my best friend. A good book can make us smarter,calmer and happier.I hope to keep reading all my life and to share my love for the books with everyone around me.I believe that if more people made reading a habit,the world would be a kinder and wiser place.
